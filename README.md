#### Featured project. Who wins in the air, and does it last?

[![Defender win rate in aerial duels by height difference](https://lipealiseda.github.io/header-quality/img/d1_height_and_duels.png)](https://lipealiseda.github.io/header-quality/)

A study of 10,327 headed shots and 95,942 aerial duels from StatsBomb open data. It builds expected models for headers in attack and defense, ranks 512 players by aerial duels won above expectation, and tests which rankings reflect real skill.

- Height decides who wins the header. A defender 10+ cm taller wins 76% of aerial duels, one 10+ cm shorter wins 39%.
- Aerial duel skill repeats from one set of matches to the next (reliability 0.67), even after accounting for both players' heights.
- Headed finishing shows no sign of skill, and free data has too few headers per player to rule it out either.
